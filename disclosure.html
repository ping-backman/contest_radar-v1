Editorial standards

Editorial standards & disclosure

We aim to publish competitions only after reviewing an organiser's source and available official rules. Listing information can change; the organiser's official terms control.

Eligibility and deadlines

Eligibility should be stated from the official rules rather than inferred. Closing dates should include the relevant time or timezone when the organiser provides it. If a listing cannot be verified, it should not be published.

Commercial relationships

This starter version does not intentionally include affiliate links. Update this page if monetisation, sponsorships or other commercial relationships are introduced. Clearly identify paid placements and affiliate links where applicable.

Not the organiser

Contest Radar is a directory and is not the organiser of listed competitions unless a listing explicitly says otherwise. Entry questions, prize fulfilment and official terms are handled by the organiser.
